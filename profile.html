Profile & Vision

Bio

I'm building toward a career in HR analytics, ESG, and digital environmental work. My background bridges Human Resources and Environmental Engineering — a Master's thesis on AI applications in HRM, and a Bachelor's project assessing environmental risk in industrial enterprises. Right now I'm strengthening that foundation through hands-on simulation work (BCG, White & Case, GE Aerospace) and an AI fluency internship at FlyRank, guided by my academic background in each area.

Multidisciplinary Bridge

Most HR analytics work treats "people data" and "environmental data" as separate disciplines. I don't. Both are ultimately about reading structured evidence — compa-ratios, emissions baselines, compliance gaps — and turning it into a decision someone with authority can act on. My Environmental Engineering background gives me the analytical discipline to trust data over instinct; my HR training gives me the judgment to know that not every number should become a policy. The intersection is where I do my best work.

"Sükut Süfrəsi" — Philosophy

Alongside HR and ESG work, I write. "Sükut Süfrəsi" ("The Table of Silence") is a multilingual philosophical dialogue exploring the space between language and silence — where words fall short and meaning has to be found in what's left unsaid. It's a different register from my analytical work, but the same underlying habit: paying close attention before drawing a conclusion.
