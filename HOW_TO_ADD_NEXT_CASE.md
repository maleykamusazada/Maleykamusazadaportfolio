# How to Add the Next Case

## Where it goes
`experience.html`, as a new `<div class="case">` block, added after the existing four cases and before the "Currently In Progress" section. Same file, same page — no new page needed.

## Next piece of work
The al-Fārābī / Spinoza philosophical article (comparing al-Fārābī's theory of emanation with Spinoza's determinism), targeting Scopus-indexed publication via Hamdard Islamicus.

## Steps to add it (reusing the Week 2 three-beat shape)

1. **Open the Claude Project** ("AI-Assisted Research & Productivity"). It already has the voice card, the identity kit, and every prior case as context — no need to re-explain any of that.
2. **Have a short interview**, one question at a time, same as every previous case:
   - The Problem: what gap or question the article addresses, and why Hamdard Islamicus specifically.
   - What I Did: the actual argument — how al-Fārābī's emanation theory is being placed against Spinoza's determinism, what the real analytical decision was.
   - What Came Of It: submission status, reviewer feedback if any, or what the draft produced.
3. **Draft the three beats** in voice-card style (serious, professional, specific, fact-based, no clichés) — same structure as the four existing cases.
4. **Get the case's HTML block** written in the same `.case` card format as the others.
5. **Add it to GitHub**: open `experience.html`, use the pencil (Edit) icon, paste the new case block in, verify `<!DOCTYPE html>` still has its `<`, commit.
6. **Check the live site** after a few minutes to confirm the new case renders and the page still loads correctly.

Total time once the work itself exists: one short Claude conversation plus one GitHub edit — not a rebuild.
