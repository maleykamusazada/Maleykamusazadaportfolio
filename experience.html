GE Aerospace — HR Job Simulation

The Problem

GE Aerospace's virtual HR program (via Forage) required practical work across three real HR functions: process improvement, feedback delivery, and compensation analysis — not theory, but tasks a working HR analyst actually does.

What I Did

I applied a Lean/Value-Added lens to a real daily process to find waste. I wrote fact-based, growth-oriented feedback using the SOI model (Standard–Observation–Impact) for a underperforming team scenario. For Compensation & Benefits, I used VLOOKUP and formulas across a real employee dataset to calculate compa-ratios and identify which employees most needed a pay increase.

What Came Of It

I completed all three tasks and earned the certificate. The compensation exercise in particular reframed how I see HR: not just policy, but a data-driven discipline where structure and empathy have to work together.

White & Case — UK ESG Job Simulation

The Problem

ESG due diligence was required on a corporate acquisition target — assessing greenwashing risk in the target company's public sustainability claims before a client proceeded with the deal.

What I Did

I flagged three red flags: vague sustainability language with litigation risk under the UK CMA Green Claims Code, reliance on carbon offsets instead of direct emission reductions, and transparency gaps masking larger non-compliance. Rather than recommend blocking the deal outright, I applied the OECD 6-step due diligence model and recommended a conditional go — proceed only if the highest-risk flags were neutralized through indemnities and carve-outs.

What Came Of It

I completed the simulation and earned the certificate. The output was a due-diligence memo with a conditional recommendation, structured to bring the target into compliance and protect the acquirer's ESG rating post-acquisition.

Mitigation strategy matrix from the due-diligence memo.

BCG — Climate & Sustainability Job Simulation

The Problem

Setting an emissions baseline for a company, then building a decarbonization roadmap — from raw data to boardroom-ready recommendations.

What I Did

I ran a carbon emissions baseline analysis and found that 86.4% of total emissions came from a single source: "Use of Sold Products" (Scope 3). I used that to prioritize the roadmap — electrification strategy first, since it hit the biggest hotspot directly, followed by supply-chain decarbonization and improved data transparency to move the company's CDP rating from B to A.

What Came Of It

I completed the simulation and earned the certificate. The concrete output was a hotspot-driven roadmap with three ranked priorities, plus a data-driven investor communication piece tied to the CDP rating gap.

FlyRank AI Internship — Workflow Audit & Portfolio Sitemap

The Problem

Week one required showing where my daily time actually goes. Separately, my portfolio's first sitemap sketch had six disconnected sections and didn't meet the brief's minimal, linear structure.

What I Did

I sorted 15 daily activities into four AI-collaboration categories and justified every decision. For the sitemap, I cut six sections down to four columns around a single home page, folding creative work into a supporting line rather than giving it its own column.

What Came Of It

Both were submitted. The audit showed I spread my time across more domains than I realized. The sitemap exercise forced a real decision about which parts of my background lead and which support — the same structure this site now uses.
