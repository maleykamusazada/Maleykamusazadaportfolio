HR Mate — AI-Powered HR Toolkit

A web app that brings together the tools an HR team reaches for daily: CV screening scored against a job description, a 200+ question interview bank organized by function, a KPI & 360° feedback framework, and a quick-reference guide to Azerbaijan's Labor Code.

What it does

Automatically scores and ranks candidate CVs (PDF/DOCX/TXT) against a job description, surfacing strengths and gaps for each candidate.

Offers a filterable bank of interview questions across 11+ specialties, plus a practical/logic-reasoning category.

Includes KPI frameworks (SMART, OKR, Balanced Scorecard) and an interactive 360° evaluation calculator.
